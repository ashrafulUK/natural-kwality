About the company

A Dhaka food company built around one word: Kwaliti.

Natural Kwaliti is a food and beverage company headquartered in Gulshan, Dhaka. The name says what the company is built on — sourcing natural ingredients and holding every batch, from a 2kg bag of rice to a bottle of mango drink, to the same standard before it reaches a shelf.

The range spans pantry staples and everyday drinks, packed for both retail shelves and bulk distribution, with the brand's green, red and gold mark appearing across every product line as a mark of consistency.
